The Problem

Three ways engineering organizations lose their minds.

42% Panopto, 2018

Knowledge drain

When a specialist leaves, their decision context, lessons learned, and failure patterns leave with them. Rebuilding takes years. Sometimes the knowledge is gone permanently.

80% Panopto / KM Research

Solved twice

A critical problem your team solved last quarter is being solved from scratch by another team right now. They will never know about each other. The same three weeks. Spent twice.

1.8 hrs McKinsey Global Institute

Connections never happen

Engineers spend 1.8 hours every day searching for information. Not because it does not exist. Because no one built the layer that connects the answer to the question.

Mainstream AI is solving the wrong problem. Your tools can summarize yesterday's meeting, draft your emails, and generate your slides. Useful for the individual. Invisible to the organization. When the conversation ends, the knowledge disappears. No one captures what was decided, why it was decided, or who in the building already knew the answer.

The Solution

Cognitive infrastructure for engineering.

memory

Capture

A thin intelligence layer wraps around everything you already use: meetings, documents, messages, decisions. It continuously converts human context into structured, AI-ready knowledge. No new tools to learn. No new habits to form. Nothing changes except that nothing gets lost.

account_tree

Structure

Raw context is continuously shaped into a cognitive topology of your organization's expertise: how decisions were made, why approaches were chosen, which problems were already solved and by whom. It deepens with every interaction.

bolt

Activate

Engineers work with organizational knowledge interactively: not just finding answers, but understanding the full reasoning behind them. Management sees which expertise is at risk, where knowledge concentrates, and what problems are being reinvented across teams. The organization gains the ability to think about itself.

We call it Narrative Encryption. Your engineering knowledge reaches frontier AI, and nothing identifiable ever leaves your perimeter. Cloud or local. Zero data exposure.
